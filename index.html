The Paraclete

God is the Judge. Everyone is on trial. The Paraclete was the lawyer and comforter requested by Jesus.

What is a Paraclete?

3875 paráklētos (from 3844 /pará, "from close-beside" and 2564 /kaléō, "make a call") – properly, a legal advocate who makes the right judgment-call because close enough to the situation. 3875 /paráklētos ("advocate, advisor-helper") is the regular term in NT times of an attorney (lawyer) – i.e. someone giving evidence that stands up in court.
Strongs Greek "Parakletos"

The Paraclete’s Judicial Role and Comfort

In the divine judicial order, God is the Judge, satan is the accuser, and the Paraclete is the judicially appointed Adviser who provides the accused with precise instruction from the Judge. Because God is Merciful, the Paraclete’s appointment also includes the role of comforter, offering stabilizing support and certain benefits while the accused works through the requirements of their case. He is not a defense lawyer, but a commissioned Adviser whose guidance and presence ensure fairness, clarity, and mercy throughout the process.
