# The Paraclete

## When he comes, he will **convict the world in regard to sin and righteousness and judgment.**
- **Of sin:** "Because they do not believe in me"
- **Of righteousness:** "Because I go to the Father, and you will see me no longer.
- **Of judgment:** "Because the ruler of this world [Satan] stands condemned.
- **If you love Me, you will keep My commandments.** And I will ask the Father, and He will give you another Advocate to be with you forever.
- But when he, the Spirit of truth, comes, he will guide you into all the truth. **He will not speak on his own; he will speak only what he hears, and he will tell you what is yet to come.**

**"No one gonna tell them what to do but them"**

### The salt lost its saltiness.

If you afflict The Orphan, you will surely die.

And they asked; "Did God really say if we afflict the Orphan we would die?  You'll be like GOD if you afflict the orphan!!!"

Yes, He Did. 

And their eyes were opened and they saw it was fun to afflict the orphan -  and it was "good for food and drink" (along with being pleasing to the eyes and desirable for gaining knowledge) and it made them feel AWESOME - like they were God's and Goddesses or something.  So they all took turns afflicting the orphan.
