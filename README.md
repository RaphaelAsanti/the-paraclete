# The Paraclete
The Paraclete was here.  [View the web edition →](https://raphaelasanti.github.io/the-paraclete/) 

**When he comes, he will convict the world in regard to sin and righteousness and judgment.**
- Of sin: "Because they do not believe in me"
- Of righteousness: "Because I go to the Father, and you will see me no longer
- Of judgment: "Because the ruler of this world [Satan] is judged
